# ExportAI Store Listing Copy (English)

## Extension name
ExportAI - AI Conversation Exporter

## Short description
Save complete AI conversations as clean files for archiving, sharing, and review.

## Full description
ExportAI is a browser extension for saving complete AI chat records. It helps you turn important conversations into files you can archive, share, review, or reuse in your research, writing, coding, operations, and client work.

Free accounts can sign in and manually export from the popup. Members unlock the in-page floating export panel, account-level settings sync, and advanced export options.

Key features:
- Works on supported AI chat pages.
- Export conversations into readable document, archive, spreadsheet, and image-style files.
- Preserves conversation structure such as code blocks, lists, tables, math, images, and attachment-related content where supported by the page.
- Select multiple formats in the popup and export with one click.
- Members can use a draggable floating export panel directly inside supported chat pages.
- Sync theme, language, export preferences, download behavior, and floating panel settings after login.
- Free accounts can use basic manual export; advanced formats and integration features require membership.

Who it is for:
- People who use AI for research, writing, coding, marketing, analysis, or support work.
- Users who need clean records of AI conversations as documents, spreadsheets, screenshots, or web archives.
- Teams and solo builders who want their export preferences synced across browser sessions.

Privacy note:
Export processing primarily happens inside the browser extension. The account server is used for login, membership status, and settings sync, not for storing your chat body. Make sure you have permission to save the conversations you export.

## Search terms
AI conversation export, chat archive, conversation backup

## Permission notes
- activeTab/tabs: detect the current supported AI chat page and read exportable conversation content.
- storage: save login state, membership status, settings, and export preferences.
- downloads: save generated files to the browser download flow.
- pageCapture/debugger: generate exports that require page rendering or capture.
- host permissions: limited to supported AI platforms and the ExportAI website for login sync.
